# Vision

> **Engineering intent should never be lost, engineering knowledge should never be
> forgotten, and engineering reality should continuously align with engineering
> decisions.**

## The problem

Engineering organisations do not fail for lack of tools. They fail because knowledge
fragments across source code, requirements, architecture, models, tests, certification
evidence, wikis, spreadsheets, tickets and the heads of individual engineers — none of
which understand each other.

As a programme evolves, reality drifts from intent. Architecture erodes, traceability
breaks, documentation goes stale, certification gets expensive, and the reasoning behind
decisions leaves with the people who made them.

## The position

Ejadah builds the layer that keeps intent and reality aligned — continuously, with
evidence, and without asking anyone to abandon the tools they already use.

We do not replace ALM, MBSE, SCM, PLM or CI. We connect them, and we answer questions
none of them can answer alone.

Stated as plainly as it can be stated:

> **Requirements tools, MBSE tools and ALM tools manage artifacts. Sanad manages and
> validates the relationships between artifacts.**

An artifact on its own is a document. A requirement matters because of what it derives from,
what implements it, what verifies it and what it affects when it changes. Those relationships
are where certification evidence comes from, where drift shows up first, and where every tool
that owns one slice of the lifecycle has nothing to say.

## What makes it defensible

Not the features. Every individual feature is implementable by a competent team with a
coding assistant.

What is hard to copy is the **methodology** — evidence-first engineering, compiled
governance, deterministic analysis with AI strictly subordinate, extraction by proof,
measurable technical debt, and single-SHA reproducibility. See
[Engineering_Principles.md](../engineering/Engineering_Principles.md).

That methodology is a strategic asset in its own right, alongside the software.

## The ten-year shape

Engineering methodologies distributed like packages. Industry standards as reusable
process packs. AI agents working under organisation-approved governance. Every artefact
contributing to a knowledge graph whose value compounds as it grows.

We reach it one proven capability at a time, inside one product — never by promising a
family of products first. Each capability is a build in progress until its verification
record is signed. See [../adr/ADR-0003-One-Product-One-Platform.md](../adr/ADR-0003-One-Product-One-Platform.md).
