Do

  • Clear space of one node diameter on every side.
  • Full mark at 48px or larger; below that, the small version.
  • Pick the variant made for the ground.

Never

  • Gradients, glows, bevels or metallic effects.
  • Rotate, stretch, outline or recolour.
  • Rebuild the mark from a screenshot or an image generator.
  • Set the two marks side by side as equals.

Deep navy, gold, cool white

Why these colours

Navy: authority

The colour of institutions trusted with consequences: aviation, banking, defence. It's also the engineer's dark-mode IDE, so site and product feel like one place.

Gold: verified

A seal, a gold standard. Used sparingly it points the eye; overused it reads as luxury or crypto. Hence the cap.

Cool white: calm

Less glare than pure white on a dark ground. Quiet confidence, not excitement.

What we avoid

Electric blue, violet, neon and gradients: the generic AI-startup look our voice already rejects.

Status: the three honesty words

build in progressplanned

The word is always shown; colour only supports it. Never red or gold for status.

06

The diagram is the argument

We show structure, evidence and real output, not people pointing at screens.

Use

  • Conceptual diagrams in node, edge, check geometry, with one gold node: the answer.
  • Engineering diagrams in the picture-theme colours.
  • Real screenshots on product surfaces, with real data.
  • Real outputs as proof: a report, a finding, a CI gate.

Never

  • Stock photos of people or offices.
  • AI illustrations, glowing brains, circuit textures.
  • Fake data presented as real.
  • Screenshots on the homepage.
